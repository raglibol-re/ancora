Logo directions
1a Rete Recommended

The rete is the rotating star map of an astrolabe. Two rings, four degree ticks, a pointer that turns to one star. The pointer finding the star is the product: your position, found.

1b Meridian

The meridian line through the horizon, with the star at its top. Clear and calm, but at small sizes it drifts toward a crosshair or a map pin.

1c Beacon

An open ring like a sextant arc, its gap held by the star; two lines of light reach up from a lamp. Warmest of the three, closest to the lighthouse, but the beam lines vanish below 24 px.
