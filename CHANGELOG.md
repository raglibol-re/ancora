# Changelog

- 2026-10-28: Payment links. Organization → Payment links asks for money as an invoice or a fee, shared as a link or QR code; payers open a public page, pay from any Stellar wallet, and the contract records who paid. New organization Wasm (interface 6).
- 2026-10-28: Landing headline renders the same in every browser: solid starlight and brass with a night halo instead of a text-clipped gradient under a drop-shadow filter, which Safari drew wrongly.
- 2026-10-28: Batch payments. Send money → Several: up to 50 payments in one asset as one request, judged on the total, approved once and paid out together (all or none). New organization Wasm `b51f089a…` (interface 5).
- 2026-10-28: Activity and statements. A new Activity page with search (name, reference, category, amount), filters (direction, asset, month), totals and a CSV of what is shown; a monthly statement per asset that prints or saves as PDF. The overview links to it.
- 2026-10-28: A signed action right after opening or reloading the app no longer fails with "Wallet not connected": it waits for the passkey to reconnect ("Reconnecting your passkey…"). Found by the end-to-end test.
- 2026-10-28: Push notifications: turn them on per device on your pass page and ancora tells you when something waits for your approval, when your request went through, was approved or declined, and when a guardian starts recovering your pass. The daily scheduler now also sends requests whose objection window has passed; `/api/execute-request` and `/api/standing-orders` became `/api/due`.
- 2026-10-28: Address book. Saved recipients shared by every member (name, address, note, last payment, Pay button) under Organization → Address book, and "Save to the address book" when paying someone new. New organization Wasm `d638ab20…` (interface 4); older organizations list paid recipients read-only.
- 2026-10-28: Custom roles. An organization adds its own roles, reorders them and removes roles nobody holds, from a Roles card on the Rules page; saved as one rule change under the usual approvals. New organization Wasm `3121d0ec…` with `SetRoles` and a `version()` view; older organizations show their roles read-only.
- 2026-10-28: Live exchange rates work again: Frankfurter moved to api.frankfurter.dev/v1 and the old address redirects without CORS, so the headline total had fallen back to the simulated rate.
- 2026-10-28: Account sheets no longer vanish when "Add money", the Reserve or "To a bank account" opens from them (a sheet stepping aside was reported as closed). The end-to-end test runs nightly in GitHub Actions.
- 2026-10-28: Production deploys again: the scheduler's two extra routes had pushed the app past Vercel Hobby's twelve functions, so every deployment since 26 Oct was refused after building. Running one order, the daily run and the organization index now share `/api/standing-orders`; a unit test keeps the count at twelve or below.
- 2026-10-28: End-to-end test of the core path on the real testnet with a virtual passkey (`pnpm e2e`): create a pass, sign out and back in, found an organization, take test money, pay. It found and led to a fix: a new pass's name could be lost when leaving the sign-in screen early.
- 2026-10-26: Roadmap gains a production assessment: what a real user app still needs against Revolut Business, Squads and Safe, in order.
- 2026-10-26: The hero headline no longer jumps while the display font loads: Fraunces is self-hosted and preloaded, and its largest size leaves room for the fallback serif. Every screen names the browser tab ("Send money · ancora"); the landing page's title describes the product.
- 2026-10-26: Standing orders run on their own: a daily cron visits every known organization and runs the due ones (also "Run due standing orders now" in the demo admin). Organizations are indexed when deployed and when opened.
- 2026-10-26: Sheets (add money, withdraw, passkey prompts) show the working status inside: a turning ring and the step's label while a passkey, a wallet or a transaction is in flight.
- 2026-10-26: A working bar under the top bar (moving line and a short status) for every on-chain step: passkey prompt, pass creation, wallet signing, every contract call, every transaction ancora's server submits. Glows instead of moving with reduced motion.
- 2026-10-26: Test money says when it can't help: the health answer carries, per asset, whether the operator's float can hand anything out, and the add-money sheet shows a note instead of the button (Circle's testnet faucet is down, so USDC and EURC floats are empty).
- 2026-10-26: Standing orders: a payment repeated weekly, fortnightly or monthly, set from the Send page; each run goes through the organization's rules, due orders run when the organization is opened or with "Run now", the owner or the top role stops them. New organization Wasm (organizations created before today don't have the feature).
- 2026-10-26: Signing in with an existing passkey works again on any device: ancora serves the smart-account index the passkey kit asks for, completing the wallet's creation transaction from the ledger where Mercury's indexer leaves it empty. Plain-language message when an account can't be confirmed yet.
- 2026-10-26: Activity now comes from the ledger: token transfers in and out of the organization (deposits, payments, Reserve moves) read from Soroban RPC events for the last week, each with its transaction; payments the ledger shows replace the request-derived entry. Addresses read shortened, payees by name.
