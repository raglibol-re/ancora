# Changelog

- 2026-10-26: Secure handover (escrow): a payment can be held after approval until the recipient and the organization both confirm (`confirm_escrow`, `cancel_escrow`, `escrow` view on the organization contract, new WASM). Send page toggle, request cards show both confirmations with a link for the recipient, `/app/escrow/<org>/<id>` lets the recipient confirm with their own pass; sample data simulates the recipient.
- 2026-10-26: Smoother shell: the top banner and the phone header stay pinned while content scrolls (the side panel sits below them), pages fade in on navigation, sheets slide up and the drawer slides in, buttons and panel items settle instead of snapping; smooth scrolling for in-page links. All motion is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- 2026-10-26: App panel reworked: brand header with divider, everyday items, a collapsible "Organization" section (approval rules, budgets, people & limits, settings) with a rotating chevron and a left-edge accent on the active item, utility links at the bottom (your pass, network & data, about). Full-height, scrolls on its own; on phones a menu button opens the same panel as a drawer next to the bottom tab bar.

Overview headline shows the total in the home currency including other assets at an indicative ECB rate, with an allocation bar (available, waiting, Reserve).

- 2026-10-26: Lifeline recovery (task 17): new `ancora_recovery` contract (plans with guardians, threshold and waiting period; claims started and confirmed by guardians, cancellable by the old pass; handover of positions to the new pass) and `recover_member` on the organization contract (new WASM). Pass page: Lifeline card, guardian card, "Recover an old pass"; app-wide warning banner while a claim is open. `/api/finish-recovery` submits the handover. Sample data simulates it (Inês is protected by Tomás and Marta).

- 2026-10-26: Bank deposits and withdrawals through a Stellar anchor (SEP-1/10/24, task 10): "From a bank account" in the add-money sheet and "To a bank account" in the asset sheet.
